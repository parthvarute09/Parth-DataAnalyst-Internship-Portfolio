## 🔑 Key Results

- **992** clean, analysis-ready orders delivered from 1,000 raw rows (Task 1)
- **36.4%** of revenue driven by the Electronics category — the single largest segment (Task 2)
- **₹139.4M** total revenue analyzed across **947 customers**; Premium Bulk Buyers are just **21%** of orders but drive **45%** of revenue (Task 3)
- **F(3, 996) = 550.63, p < 0.001** — ANOVA confirms customer segment spending differences are statistically significant, not chance (Task 4)
